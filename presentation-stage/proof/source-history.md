# Where our music material came from

## Online video and audio: Wikimedia Commons pilot
The pilot inventory records five source recordings: two dombra-only videos, one vocal-and-dombra field video, and two vocal anthem recordings. Source inventory status does not mean every recording became a training crop.

- Boz salkin: an online dombra performance video, recorded as CC BY 3.0 on Commons. This is Kyrgyz repertoire, not evidence that every pilot item was Kazakh repertoire.
- Moon and stars: an own-work video from Kazakhstan, recorded as CC BY-SA 4.0.
- Singing in the Yurt at Nowruz: a field video recorded as CC BY 2.0. Missing aligned lyrics limited vocal training use.
- Two anthem recordings had time-aligned Kazakh lyrics and were recorded as public domain in the source inventory.

We investigated other online videos too. Finding a video did not mean it entered training: unresolved performer rights, unsuitable instrumentation or missing lyrics could exclude it. Direct YouTube searches and candidate archive footage should not be described as accepted training material.
