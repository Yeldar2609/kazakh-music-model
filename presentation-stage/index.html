OUR BUILD JOURNEY / TWO DIFFERENT DATA PROBLEMS

FIRST THE DOMBRA.
THEN THE VOICE.

01

Listen and classify

We reviewed 116 archive recordings by ear: solo dombra, voice with dombra, or orchestra. Labels alone were not enough.

02

Instrumental expansion

We used 59 dombra-only recordings to create 111 new instrumental crops. This stage focused on the sound of the instrument.

03

A separate vocal workflow

Vocals also needed words aligned to each clip. We held 27 dombra-and-voice recordings back while their lyrics remained unverified.

These were successive experiments and separate preparation tasks, not two models whose outputs we mixed together. The earlier pilot already contained a few vocal crops.

THE ENGINEERING / MORE THAN PRESSING GENERATE

THE HARD PARTS.
DATA, LANGUAGE, MEMORY.

01

Checking every source

We found online videos and public datasets, checked provenance and usage terms, extracted suitable audio crops, then checked decoding, silence, clipping and duplicates.

02

Making vocals usable

Automatic lyric drafts were not enough. Singing needs the right words in the right crop. Kazakh text had to survive processing unchanged.

03

Fitting the laptop

We used four-bit base weights, a small adapter and gradient checkpointing to fit the final run on a 4 GB GPU.

We estimate 300+ minutes across training experiments. Listening, source research, filtering, preparation, troubleshooting and generation added further work; total project time was not measured.

ALTERNATIVE WE INVESTIGATED

VocalRender: score-driven singing

Requires words, MIDI pitches and note durations, plus tempo. Its documented datasets include Mandarin singing. It produces the vocal line; accompaniment needs a separate path.

For our recordings: this would require detailed note and syllable annotation.

OUR IMPLEMENTED PATH

ACE-Step: prompt + lyrics → music

We supply a description, Kazakh lyrics and duration. The model generates singing and accompaniment. We did not write a note-by-note score.

Our configuration: 30 seconds, fixed seed, 8 generation steps. BPM and key left unspecified; automatic prompt rewriting disabled.
